Parking Alert

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2, 2026

Welcome to Parking Alert ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use the Parking Alert mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our vehicle notification and parking assistance services, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Phone number and basic profile details collected during authentication to create and verify your secure account.
  • Vehicle Details: Vehicle registration numbers added by vehicle owners to facilitate emergency parking notifications and alerts.
  • Communication Data: In-app parking alert notifications, quick messages, and chat history exchanged between vehicle owners and searchers regarding parked vehicles.
  • Device & Notification Identifiers: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens and device information used exclusively to deliver push notifications when someone alerts you about your parked vehicle.

2. Real-Time In-App Voice Calling

Microphone & Audio Privacy: The application requests access to your microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) strictly for real-time encrypted in-app voice calling via WebRTC. We do not record, store, or transmit audio recordings to our servers. Voice audio streams are transmitted in real-time between participants and terminated immediately when the call ends.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely for the following purposes:

  • To enable users to alert vehicle owners about improper parking or vehicle emergencies.
  • To deliver instant, high-priority push notifications to vehicle owners.
  • To enable real-time encrypted voice calls and in-app chat between vehicle searchers and owners.
  • To manage ad-free reward passes earned by users who choose to view rewarded advertisements.
  • To maintain app security, prevent invalid activity, and troubleshoot technical errors.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Our application integrates trusted third-party services that may collect information used to identify you in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: Core platform support and in-app updates.
  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, app open, and rewarded ads. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, advertising IDs, and usage analytics to serve relevant and compliant ads.
  • Firebase (Google LLC): We use Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for secure account login, encrypted message storage, and push notifications.

5. Data Security

We employ industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures, including HTTPS encryption for all network traffic and WebRTC DTLS/SRTP encryption for real-time media, to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to provide the services requested. You have the right to request deletion of your account and associated vehicle records at any time directly through the app settings or by contacting our support team. Upon your request, your personal data will be permanently purged from our servers.

7. Children's Privacy

Parking Alert is intended for vehicle owners, drivers, and adult users. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will immediately delete such information.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. You are advised to review this page periodically for any updates.
